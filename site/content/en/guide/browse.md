Once connected (or with the card plugged in), OM Photos lists the files on your card, much like the Photos app.

## By date

Photos are grouped by capture date, newest first. Each thumbnail has a type label in the bottom-left corner:

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| JPG | JPG only |
| RAW | RAW (.ORF) only |
| RAW+JPG | The RAW and JPG of the same shot, shown as one |
| MOV / MP4 ▶ 0:12 | A video and its length |

A green arrow in the top-left corner marks photos you already imported; a lock in the top-right marks files protected on the camera.

Change the thumbnail size with the slider in the top-right corner of the window, or pinch on the trackpad.

## Filters

- **Type**: All, Photos, RAW and Videos in the sidebar, with counts.
- **Import status**: All, Not imported and Imported at the top of the window.
- **Date**: click a date in the sidebar to jump to that day.

## Selecting

| Action | How |
|---|---|
| Select one | Click |
| Add or remove | ⌘-click |
| Select a range | Click the first, Shift-click the last |
| Select all | ⌘A |
| Clear | Esc |

With photos selected, the bottom bar shows how many and their total size, with Import and Delete buttons.

## Full-size view

Double-click or press Space to open a photo:

- Left and right arrow keys go to the previous and next photo.
- Press `i` for the file names, sizes and capture time; videos also show length and resolution.
- Import or delete this photo with the buttons at the bottom.
- Esc or Space closes it.

Videos play after you import them (the camera cannot stream them).

## Camera information

The top of Settings lists the model, firmware version, lens, free space on the card and the number of files. Firmware and lens are read from the newest photo's capture information.

Your OM System camera creates its own Wi-Fi network, and OM Photos reads your photos through it. Pair once so the app remembers the camera's Wi-Fi name and password.

## 1. Show the QR code on the camera

On the camera, open **Menu › Device Connection** (on the OM-3; other models call it "Connection to Smartphone"). The screen shows a QR code with the Wi-Fi SSID and password below it.

## 2. Scan it in OM Photos

1. Open OM Photos and click **Pair Camera** (配對相機) (it opens by itself the first time).
2. Hold the camera screen in front of your Mac's camera. Pairing is done as soon as the code is read.

If scanning does not work:

- **Read from a photo** (從照片讀取): take a photo of the camera screen with your phone and choose it in OM Photos.
- **Enter manually**: switch to "Manual" (手動輸入) and type the Wi-Fi SSID and password shown on the camera.

The camera's Wi-Fi password is kept in this Mac's keychain and never uploaded.

## 3. Connect

Click **Connect** (連線). OM Photos will:

1. Remember the Wi-Fi network you are on.
2. Switch to the camera's Wi-Fi and, once connected, list the photos on the card.

If your Mac is already on the camera's Wi-Fi, OM Photos shows your photos right away, **no pairing needed**. Pairing only lets the app switch networks for you.

## 4. Switch back when you are done

Choose "⋯ › Disconnect" (中斷連線) in the top-right corner. OM Photos disconnects from the camera and switches back to the network it remembered. Quitting the app does the same.

To disconnect automatically after an import, turn on "Disconnect and switch back to my Wi-Fi after importing" (匯入完成後自動中斷並切回原本的 Wi-Fi) in Settings › Connection. It is off by default so you can keep deleting photos after an import.

## Good to know

- The camera's Wi-Fi has no internet, so your Mac is offline while connected. For lots of files, [use USB-C](/en/guide/usb/), which is more than ten times faster.
- The camera may turn its Wi-Fi off after a while. Open Device Connection again and click Connect.

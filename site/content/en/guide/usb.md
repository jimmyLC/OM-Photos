The camera's Wi-Fi is convenient but slow, about 3 MB/s. With a USB-C cable we measured 35–45 MB/s on a Mac: a one-minute 4K video goes from minutes to seconds.

## 1. Connect and choose a USB mode

Connect the camera to your Mac with a USB-C cable and turn it on. The camera asks which USB mode to use:

| Option on the camera | How OM Photos sees it | Measured speed (Mac) |
|---|---|---|
| **Storage** | The card mounts like a USB drive | about 44 MB/s |
| **MTP** | The camera appears through the system's image capture | about 35 MB/s |

Both work. Start with **Storage**, which is fully tested with OM Photos on an OM-3; MTP support was added more recently.

## 2. OM Photos switches to it

OM Photos detects the connection by itself. The sidebar shows the card (Storage mode, for example "OM SYSTEM") or the camera's name (MTP mode) and lists your photos. No pairing, no Wi-Fi.

If OM Photos is already connected to the camera over Wi-Fi, it stays there; click the USB source in the sidebar to switch.

## 3. Unplug when done

In Storage mode, eject the card in Finder before unplugging. OM Photos then returns to the Wi-Fi source.

## One import history

Whether you import over Wi-Fi, USB Storage or MTP, OM Photos remembers each file by its card path, size and capture time, so switching connections never imports the same photo twice.

## iPhone and iPad (coming soon)

iPhone 15 and later, and iPads with USB-C, can connect to the camera with a cable too. Steps will be added when the app ships.

Once your files are safely on your Mac, you can clear them off the camera card from OM Photos.

> Deleting **cannot be undone**. The card has no trash.

## Delete several photos

1. Select the photos to delete (⌘-click, Shift-click for a range, ⌘A for all).
2. Click **Delete** (刪除) at the bottom, press the Delete key, or right-click a photo and choose "Delete from Camera…" (從相機刪除…).
3. The confirmation lists the file names and **warns about any you have not imported yet**. Check, then click "Delete".

The RAW and JPG of the same shot are deleted together. When it finishes, the bottom bar says how many were deleted and whether any failed, and the list updates.

## Delete the photo you are viewing

In the full-size view, click "Delete". After you confirm, it moves on to the next photo, handy for going through photos one by one.

## Protected files

Files protected on the camera (lock icon in the top-right corner) are not deleted; the confirmation says how many will be skipped. Remove the protection on the camera first if you want to delete them.

## Works over Wi-Fi and USB

Over Wi-Fi OM Photos uses the camera's delete command; in USB Storage and MTP modes it deletes the files on the card directly. Wi-Fi and USB Storage have been tested on an OM-3.
